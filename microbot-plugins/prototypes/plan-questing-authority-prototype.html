PlanQuesting authority prototype

This throwaway demo asks whether a quest can safely move between deterministic automation, one bounded agent recovery, and player control without two actors acting at once or an agent bypassing a dangerous-content gate.

Prototype only. It does not connect to RuneLite, an agent, or the Wiki. State lives in this page and disappears on refresh.
